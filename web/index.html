The entire system

  1. Every month, the day cash arrives: invest it. Never wait for a dip. Perfect entry timing was measured to be worth +0.63% over 33 years. Waiting costs ~2.8 bp per day.
  2. Signal ON — SPX above its 200-day average: buy 70% CL2 · 30% LQQ. Hold everything already invested. Signal OFF — SPX below it: this month’s cash and all holdings go to the money-market ETF, until the price recrosses.
  3. Check the signal once a month, on deposit day. That is all. Historically ~2 switches per year. Buy late morning with a limit at mid; never the closing auction; skip the day if the spread exceeds 25 bp.
